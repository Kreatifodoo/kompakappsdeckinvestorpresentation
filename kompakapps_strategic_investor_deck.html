Market Friction • Structural Problem

Heavy back-office ERPs fail at the factory floor, isolating 80% of enterprise labor

Traditional operational tooling creates data blind spots, reconciliation overhead, and rampant fraud.

Slide 04 / 22

Biometric Terminal Frailties

Fingerprint hardware breaks frequently, requires local server wiring, and fails completely for offsite, mobile, or roving workers.

Chaotic Paper Forms & Excel

Leave approvals, expense receipts, and overtime are scribbled on physical slips, causing endless payroll disputes and re-entry lag.

Cloud-Only Apps Stall in Field

Standard white-collar SaaS apps crash when staff enter basement warehouses, concrete factories, or remote sites without reliable 4G.

ERP Licences Prohibitive

Core ERP seat licenses ($30–$70/mo) are economically unfeasible and too complex to roll out to temporary or blue-collar frontline staff.

The KompakApps Intervention:

KompakApps unifies frontline mobile workflows into a single offline-capable mobile operating system that syncs seamlessly back into corporate ERP records, eliminating manual re-entry and terminal maintenance.
